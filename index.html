i.

ii.

The bar is the bottleneck

Rounds takes the transaction out of the bottleneck entirely.

02 — How it works

Three steps. A few seconds.

i.

Order

Guests choose their drink and pay on their phone, tip included, before they reach the bar.

ii.

Scan

Staff confirm the prepaid order with a single scan on their own device.

iii.

Pour

The order posts to the POS as a paid check. The drink is handed over. Done.

05 — Questions

Everything else, answered.

01Does Rounds replace our point-of-sale?
No. Rounds runs alongside the POS you already use. Orders placed through Rounds arrive in your system as completed, paid checks, so your POS stays the single source of truth for sales, reporting, and end-of-night reconciliation. Nothing you currently rely on changes.
02Which POS systems does Rounds work with?
Rounds integrates directly with Toast first — the most widely used point-of-sale in bars and nightlife. The platform is built so additional POS systems can be added over time. If you run Toast, you're ready to go.
03Do our bartenders have to learn a new system?
No — and this is the point. Your bartenders keep working in the POS they already know. A Rounds order appears the same way any other order would, already paid. There's no parallel system to manage mid-rush, which is exactly where earlier attempts at this failed and Rounds doesn't.
04What hardware do we need to run it?
Almost none. Staff confirm orders on a phone or tablet your team likely already has. There's no proprietary terminal to install and no expensive equipment to buy. Getting started is a software setup, not a hardware project.
05How does a guest start using Rounds — is there an app to download?
No download required. Guests scan a code at the venue, which opens Rounds in their phone's browser. First-time setup takes about two minutes — name, a payment method, and a default tip. After that, placing an order takes seconds.
06How are payments processed and paid out to us?
Guests pay securely in the app at the moment they order, gratuity included. Funds settle to the venue through standard, regulated payment infrastructure and are paid out on a regular schedule, just like your existing card payments. Because every order is paid up front, there are no open tabs left to settle at close.
07How are tips handled, and who do they go to?
Gratuity is built into the order when the guest pays, with sensible defaults that encourage generous tipping. Tips are attributed to the order and flow to your staff through your normal tip process — your pooling or payout setup doesn't have to change.
08How does Rounds reduce walkouts and chargebacks?
Payment is captured before the drink is handed over, so the classic open-tab walkout simply can't happen — there's nothing left unpaid to walk out on. And because every transaction is a clean, itemized, guest-authorized charge, disputes are far easier to defend than a manually run tab.
09Is guest payment information secure?
Yes. Payment details are encrypted and tokenized through regulated, PCI-compliant payment infrastructure. Your staff never see or handle a guest's card, and sensitive payment data never sits behind your bar.
10Does Rounds handle age verification?
Rounds does not replace your legal responsibility to verify age. Your staff check ID at the point of service exactly as they do today before handing over any drink. Rounds handles ordering and payment; your team keeps full control over compliance.
11Can guests buy a round for friends or split an order?
Yes. A guest can order for themselves or cover a round for the group in a single order. The social "I've got this one" moment works the way it always has — just without anyone fumbling for a card or doing math at the bar.
12What if a guest's phone dies, or they don't want to use it?
Rounds is an addition, never a replacement. Any guest who doesn't have it, can't use it, or simply prefers the traditional way orders exactly as they always have. It's never all-or-nothing, and your bar never turns anyone away.
13Will this slow us down on a busy night?
The opposite — it's built for high-volume nights specifically. Because the guest has already chosen and paid before they reach the bar, the slowest part of the transaction is gone. Your staff spend their time pouring and serving, not taking orders and running cards.
14What reporting or insights do owners get?
Owners get a clear view of what's actually happening: sales, tips, your busiest windows, and top-selling items, summarized automatically. Most bars run on a single end-of-night total — Rounds gives you the detail to staff, stock, and plan with confidence.
15What does it cost, and how do we get started?
Pricing depends on your venue and volume, and it's designed to pay for itself through faster service and recovered walkout revenue. The best next step is a short conversation and a live look at how it works in your room — get in touch and we'll set it up.
